DESK NOTE

The note shows in amber under the eyes. Tap the screen or wave over its top edge to dismiss it.

TASKS

NODE chirps once and keeps it, out of sight, until you ask. Hold a finger on the screen to see your tasks; tap one for its details and to mark it done. Up to 30 at a time.

CAMERA

The front camera, small and live, only while this is showing and this tab is in view; switching away turns it off. Nothing is recorded, on the phone or anywhere else. It crosses your Wi-Fi as plain HTTP, like everything on this page, and the phone's screen does not show that anyone is watching.

NODE reads only the event name, session id, project folder, notification type and whether a failure was you interrupting. Your prompts and tool output are sent over plain HTTP on your Wi-Fi, so keep this to a network you trust.

SESSION %

How much of your 5-hour Claude session limit is used, as /usage shows it, in the top-left corner of the screen. Claude Code hands it only to a status line script, on Pro and Max plans, so add these lines to yours, after it reads its input into $input. They send the percentage and when it resets, nothing else.

PC LOAD

This computer's CPU, memory and disk activity, each a small icon and a number, dim in the bottom-left corner of the screen for as long as this runs. Paste it into a PowerShell window and leave it open. It sends those three numbers every 5 seconds and nothing else; stop it and they are gone from the screen within 15 seconds.
