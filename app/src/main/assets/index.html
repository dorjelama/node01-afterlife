NODE COMPANION

The face on your desk. What it sees stays on the phone unless you open the camera view below.

DESK NOTE

The note shows in amber under the eyes. Tap the screen or wave over its top edge to dismiss it.

TASKS

NODE chirps once and keeps it, out of sight, until you ask. Hold a finger on the screen to see your tasks; tap one for its details and to mark it done. Up to 30 at a time.

WEBCAM

Open the webcam

The phone is one or the other. As a webcam the face goes dark, the rear camera and the microphone stream to the webcam page, and clips are recorded to the phone; notes and alerts wait until it is the face again. Holding a finger on the phone's screen also brings the face back.

CAMERA

The front camera, small and live, only while this is showing and this tab is in view; switching away turns it off. Nothing is recorded, on the phone or anywhere else. It crosses your Wi-Fi as plain HTTP, like everything on this page, and the phone's screen does not show that anyone is watching.

CAMERA ACTIVITIES

    Off until you tick one. Then NODE looks at about one camera frame a second, on the phone, only for these, and only while you are at the desk; no frame is kept or sent. When one happens it reacts, chirps and adds a line here. The log stays on the phone for a week.

    AQUARIUM

      Say "I fed the fish", "water change done", "cleaned the filter", "trimmed the plants" or "added fertiliser", or press Done here. An overdue job shows in amber and is read out once a day while you are at the desk; "dismiss" puts it off until tomorrow. "How is the tank?" gets an answer from all of this. The water's state is a guess from your care habits: with no test kit, NODE never knows ammonia or nitrate. The log stays on the phone for a week.

      FACE

      Animations. NODE also plays these by itself: hearts when you say thank you, a happy bounce when Claude finishes, woozy when you shake it, Z's while it sleeps, and reading, thinking and narrowing its eyes while Claude works.

      CLAUDE CODE

      Add these hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json on the PC, merged into any hooks you already have. Each runs in the background and gives up after 2 seconds, so a sleeping phone never slows Claude down. The address is filled in for this phone.

      NODE reads only the event name, session id, project folder, notification type and whether a failure was you interrupting. Your prompts and tool output are sent over plain HTTP on your Wi-Fi, so keep this to a network you trust.

      SESSION %

      How much of your 5-hour Claude session limit is used, as /usage shows it, in the top-left corner of the screen. Claude Code hands it only to a status line script, on Pro and Max plans, so add these lines to yours, after it reads its input into $input. They send the percentage and when it resets, nothing else.

      PC LOAD

      This computer's CPU, memory and disk activity, each a small icon and a number, dim in the bottom-left corner of the screen for as long as this runs. Paste it into a PowerShell window and leave it open. It sends those three numbers every 5 seconds and nothing else; stop it and they are gone from the screen within 15 seconds.
