NODE AQUARIUM

Your tank, as the phone's rear camera watches it: its care, its days, and what it collects for training.

CARE

Day by day, as the rear camera measured it (moving at once is at most the fish seen together, not a count):

      Say "I fed the fish", "water change done", "cleaned the filter", "trimmed the plants" or "added fertiliser", or press Done here. An overdue job shows in amber and is read out once a day while you are at the desk; "dismiss" puts it off until tomorrow. "How is the tank?" gets an answer from all of this. The water's state is a guess from your care habits: with no test kit, NODE never knows ammonia or nitrate. The log stays on the phone for a week.

      While the tank's light is on, the rear camera watches it the whole time, beside the face's camera: a picture and its numbers every minute, and video with no sound. Live view shows it here while this tab is in view. When the light has been off a few minutes the rear camera stops, and a quick look every 10 minutes notices it coming back on.

      TRAINING

      What NODE keeps for training a model of your own later: a picture a minute while the tank is lit (60 days), its numbers, the AI's descriptions, and what you write here about the newest picture, above. Say what is really there or what is wrong: "no yellow fish; a loach on the sand, two neon tetras; the water is clear". It is kept beside that picture, and the latest one is what "how is the tank?" believes over the AI.

        On the phone: Android/data/dev.node01.companion/files/training/ (a folder a day, minutes.csv, labels.jsonl) and files/tank-video/ (about the last three days). Copy them off with adb pull.
